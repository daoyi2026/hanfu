## Search portals and catalog vocabulary

| Portal | Direct link | Suggested use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hanfu archive supplied by the user (community index) | [120.25.237.190/hanfu](http://120.25.237.190/hanfu) | Potential index for garment forms and excavation reports. It did not connect during this research pass, so it was not used as a verified factual source. |
| 中华珍宝馆 | [g2.ltfc.net](https://g2.ltfc.net/) | Find high-resolution transmitted paintings. Confirm attribution, copy date, and collection record before using a visible garment detail. |
| 故宫名画记 | [minghuaji.dpm.org.cn](https://minghuaji.dpm.org.cn/) | Interactively inspect Palace Museum paintings and notes; retain qualifiers such as “傳” and “摹”. |
| 故宫博物院数字文物库 | [dpm.org.cn/explore/collections.html](https://www.dpm.org.cn/explore/collections.html) | Search garments, textiles, headwear, and ornaments using the museum's catalog terms. Page access was limited during this pass. |
| 中国丝绸博物馆 | [chinasilkmuseum.com](https://www.chinasilkmuseum.com/) | Prioritize excavated garments, textile structures, measurements, conservation reports, and set relationships. |
| 中国国家博物馆 | [chnmuseum.cn](https://www.chnmuseum.cn/) | Search collection records for figurines, portraits, clothing, headwear, and hair ornaments. |

Catalog terms vary by institution. Useful Chinese search terms include `织绣`, `袍`, `衫`, `褶`, `裙`, `霞帔`, `命妇冠服`, `女立俑`, `仕女图`, `乐舞俑`, `加彩陶俑`, `簪`, `钗`, `扁方`, `步摇`, `梳篦`, `金钿`, and `䯼髻`. Search results still need to be checked for date, object identity, social context, and image rights.

## Generated illustrations

The current gallery uses 27 AI-generated illustrations in `assets/paintings/sets/`: three scene-specific pairs for each of nine periods, with one woman and one man in every image. The shared pale-silk painting treatment keeps the series visually consistent, while the activities and poses vary across formal, market, and travel or riding scenes. Per-image clothing anchors and reference links are listed in `IMAGE-PROMPTS.md` and `provenance.json`.

The earlier nine paired illustrations in the root of `assets/paintings/` are retained as style references; they are not the current gallery images. Generated illustrations are visual interpretations, not exact archaeological reconstructions or garment patterns, and they do not grant permission to reuse source institutions' photographs.
